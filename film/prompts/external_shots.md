# External generation shot sheet (v4 "orbital machine" film)

Make these yourself in any video tool (Kling, Veo, Seedance, Runway, Hailuo, etc.).
Upload the finished files here, named exactly as shown in the Name column.

## Specs for every clip
- 16:9, 1920x1080 if the tool offers it (1280x720 also works), 24 fps, 6 seconds.
- No on-screen text, logos, watermarks or subtitles. Sound is not used: the film keeps the GoT song bit-exact.
- No real or famous people's faces. Characters are brass automatons.
- One continuous shot each, slow camera move, subject centred, plain dark edges (each clip becomes a brass-framed panel inside the machine).

## Style prefix (paste before every prompt)
Sepia brass-and-parchment style, macro miniature diorama, polished and tarnished brass clockwork, visible cogs and planetary gears, warm low golden light from one side, deep brown shadows, shallow depth of field, film grain.

## What happens after upload
1. Each clip is checked: on-model character, hands, no stray text or watermark, no flicker over 3 flashes per second.
2. Clips that pass become brass-framed panels in the machine chambers, graded to the film palette.
3. Clips that fail are cut; the slot falls back to the code-rendered machine shot.
